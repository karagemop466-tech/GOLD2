1. Nominal fine-gold content at the dated reference

Method. Nominal fine gold = published metal weight × karat ÷ 24. Theoretical melt = nominal fine gold ÷ 31.1034768 g per troy ounce × $4,024.45/oz (header quote date unconfirmed — F-026). Karat is the seller's stated alloy, not an assay. Weight must exclude stones and non-gold components for this arithmetic to mean anything about gold content.

  • 07Physical verification. 0 of 20 rings were weighed, XRF-tested, or inspected in person (F-024).

5. What this analysis must not be used for

The melt figures are conditional arithmetic, not appraisals, offers, or resale floors. A ring can legitimately cost several times its metal value because of design, labour, setting, brand and margin — and a suspiciously low price is the direction that deserves the extra questions (checklist item: unusually cheap pricing is a red flag). Nothing here establishes that a ring is solid gold, that its stated karat is accurate, or that a seller will honour a return.
