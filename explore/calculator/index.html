The method

01 / MASS

Net alloy grams × fineness

For a nominal scenario, fineness = karat ÷ 24. Use an explicitly sourced decimal purity when supplied; do not silently replace 0.9999 with 1.

FTC karat guidance ↗
02 / UNITS

One troy ounce = 31.1034768 g

USD per fine-gold gram = USD per troy ounce ÷ 31.1034768. Do not use the ordinary avoirdupois ounce.

03 / LIMITS

Calculation ≠ authentication

A seller’s statement or a surface-only XRF reading does not establish the whole item’s composition. Ask an independent professional about an appropriate assessment.
