How this record was checked

01 / SOURCE REVIEW

Identity before numbers

Directly retrieve the official product, verify its identity and scope, and record the relevant passage. Search snippets are discovery leads only. Partial/not-found pages stay flagged.

02 / PROVENANCE RULES

Every value needs a reference

Nullable field schemas, allowed publisher domains, source/passage references, numeric price/weight consistency, currency context and timestamps are checked. These checks do not independently establish factual truth.

03 / SAFE PUBLICATION

Repeat the whole pass

Unit, internal-link, browser, accessibility and static-security tests run alongside dependency auditing. Source reachability monitoring reports problems without rewriting price data. Human source review remains necessary.

Observation timing: most source records were transcribed as a batch after fetches in the recorded window. A later recheck is recorded separately where performed. Research timestamps are not seller-supplied effective dates. Product price snapshots are flagged stale after 24 hours, not silently renewed.

Scope and integration: the initial two-setting dashboard, its sources and its tools are preserved at the original dashboard. Its Blue Nile pages were directly rechecked for the combined 18-entry catalog; unsupported currency is now flagged. See the repository review log for integration/security limits.
