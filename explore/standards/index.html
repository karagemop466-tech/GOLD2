The buying checklist

The owner’s acceptance rules, supported by official context. These rules are not claims that a seller has passed.

01

Confirm the karat

Require a stated karat and confirm the actual purity independently. Karat is parts out of 24; a stamp is not an assay.

02

Require explicit solid gold

Reject plated, vermeil, gold-filled, overlay and gold-tone. Require explicit solid-gold wording for the exact configuration.

03

Look for both marks

Require the karat hallmark and maker/manufacturer mark on the candidate ring. Neither alone proves actual purity.

04

Get the grams, with a definition

Obtain exact-size weight in grams and separate gold-alloy-only mass excluding stones, platinum and other components. Item weight is not automatically metal weight.

THE NO-HALLUCINATIONS POLICY

Never fill a gap with a plausible answer.

  1. Keep unsupported values null in data and blank in numeric fields. Flag them clearly.
  2. Link each product and substantive finding to its official source. Separate retailer claims, independent opinions and physical verification.
  3. Never infer karat from a title/URL, currency from a dollar sign, solid construction from a photograph, or net gold mass from item/shipping weight.
  4. Preserve dated price scope and conflicts. Do not call static snapshots “live.” Automated reachability checks cannot refresh a price or prove its accuracy.
  5. After every review: recheck the affected official sources, run the evidence/consistency and security passes, and publish unresolved issues. Repeat after combining parallel work.
  6. Automated checks cannot prove every claim true, eliminate all hallucinations, or replace a professional physical assessment.
