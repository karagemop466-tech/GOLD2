Quince oval 1ct

Quince uses a platinum head with the gold band

The page offers a 14k gold band with a platinum head, or all platinum. Whole-ring grams would not be an acceptable gold-alloy-only mass. Explicit solid-gold wording and component weights were not confirmed.

Next action: Request component-specific weights and construction details. Never count the platinum head or diamond as gold.

Quince return policy

365-day banner does not apply to engagement rings

The engagement-ring exception is 60 days from purchase, not 365 days from delivery. Made-to-order jewelry is subject to a $35 restocking fee; packaging and certificates matter.

Next action: Get the exact order’s eligibility, resizing/custom exclusions, refund method and inspection permission in writing before paying.

Automic Gold return policy

Return-policy mail link contains a typo

The displayed care@automicgold.com text points to a mailto address ending .om in the fetched return policy. The official Contact page separately publishes care@automicgold.com. The policy also says to mail within 14 days to avoid a recycling fee, despite its 30-day return window.

07

Automic Gold 24k Band

24k softness is a practical trade-off

The seller states 99.99% purity and warns the ring can bend, warp or scratch easily. Nominal 24/24 is not the same calculation input as a seller-declared 0.9999 fineness.

Next action: Consider daily-wear suitability with an independent jeweler. Neither purity wording nor a numerical calculation is a physical assay.

Menē Rose and Knot

Crafted-on-Demand price and weight can change

The reviewed pages show USD amounts but no primary-product grams or bound size. Terms permit up to 1g variation for Crafted-on-Demand pieces and a final-price adjustment. Next action: Get the final size, weight, total price and applicable return policy before ordering. Buyback is a separate, conditional transaction, not a guaranteed recovery of purchase price.
