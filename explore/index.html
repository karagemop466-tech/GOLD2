What this research does — and does not — establish

44 unique named product pages across 13 retailers remain covered. On 2026-10-23, all 17 records with assigned USD prices were re-read at date-level precision; 15 product amounts matched their stored display, two Menē amounts were corrected, and the WGC moving header was independently confirmed. Other fields retain their earlier source dates; this is not a full catalog recertification.

Official retail pages document seller claims, not actual metal purity. Five independent review channels were examined, but seller trustworthiness is not certified. No seller was contacted and no physical ring was tested. Recheck for hallucinations and source changes after every review.
