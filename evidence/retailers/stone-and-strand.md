## What this verifies

- Return window: 100 days from arrival (U.S. customers only).
- Return shipping is **not free**: $12.50 fee for refund to original payment method ($5 for store credit).
- Custom exclusion: **yes** — personalized (custom/engraved/monogrammed) and made-to-order pieces are final sale.
- Final-sale rule: anything marked final sale on the product page, or discounted 50% or more, cannot be
  returned or exchanged. All three Stone and Strand rings captured in this pass are marked final sale;
  two of them (60% off, 50% off) also fail on the discount rule alone (flag F-016).
- Card tender: the page refers to refunds "to the original payment method" and to "your credit card provider".
  Card networks are not named on the retrieved page.
- Contact email: care@stoneandstrand.com (published on this page).
