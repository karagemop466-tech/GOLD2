F-011 · Zales online returns depend on where resizing was performed highpolicy

The official return policy allows 30-day returns and excludes custom designs and jewelry altered/modified beyond sizing or stone setting. The separate official ring-sizer PDF says rings purchased and sized from the online store can be returned/exchanged, but rings resized at a Zales store, another retailer, or an independent jeweler are not accepted for online refund/exchange. This is not a blanket “all resized rings” rule; the location/channel matters.

Recommended action: Before any resize, obtain written confirmation for the exact item and purchase channel. Do not use an independent jeweller for resizing before the return decision.
