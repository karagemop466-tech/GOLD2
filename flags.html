F-001 · Target-product specifications do not establish explicit solid gold high checklist gap

For all 16 original-register targets, fetched target descriptions/specifications did not establish explicit solid-gold construction. A related product or general page can mention solid gold without proving this exact ring. This absence is not proof the item is plated.

Recommended action: Ask each seller in writing to confirm 'solid gold, not plated or filled' and keep the reply with the order record.

F-002 · No listing states a hallmark or maker's mark high checklist gap

The checklist asks for a 10K/14K/18K stamp PLUS a maker's/manufacturer's mark. None of the 16 product pages states what is stamped inside the band, and none names a manufacturer's mark. Blue Nile and VRAI do publish stock/style numbers, which is not the same thing as a physical hallmark.

Recommended action: On arrival, photograph the inside of the band and have an independent jeweller read the stamp and maker's mark under magnification.
