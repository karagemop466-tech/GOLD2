No-hallucination policy, applied. These are the values that were deliberately not filled in. The strongest pattern: 16 of 16 listings state a karat but none states the words “solid gold”, none states a hallmark or maker's mark, and 15 of 16 never publish a gram weight. That is a gap in the listings, not proof the rings are bad — but it is exactly the gap an independent jeweller visit closes.

F-001 · No listing uses the words "solid gold" high checklist gap

The buying checklist requires the listing itself to say 'solid gold', not plated / vermeil / gold-filled / overlay / gold-tone. Across all 16 captured listings, the retrieved page text states a karat (10K/14K/18K) but never uses the phrase 'solid gold'. Absence was checked in the retrieved page text for each item on 2026-10-22. This is a documentation gap, not proof that the ring is plated.

Recommended action: Ask each seller in writing to confirm 'solid gold, not plated or filled' and keep the reply with the order record.

F-002 · No listing states a hallmark or maker's mark high checklist gap

The checklist asks for a 10K/14K/18K stamp PLUS a maker's/manufacturer's mark. None of the 16 product pages states what is stamped inside the band, and none names a manufacturer's mark. Blue Nile and VRAI do publish stock/style numbers, which is not the same thing as a physical hallmark.

Recommended action: On arrival, photograph the inside of the band and have an independent jeweller read the stamp and maker's mark under magnification.

F-015 · Setting-only prices and complete-ring prices sit in the same tables high comparability

8 Blue Nile rows, the Brilliant Earth row and the VRAI row are 'Setting Price' / 'Setting Only' - the center diamond is extra. The 4 Zales rows are complete rings including diamonds. A $1,000 setting and a $900 complete ring are not comparable, and price-per-gram cannot be computed where the diamond dominates. Every row carries a price_basis label so the two groups can be separated.

Recommended action: Compare setting prices with setting prices only; add a centre-stone quote before comparing with a complete ring.

Affected rows: BN-203348, BN-195639, BN-194548, BE-121H6, BN-192506, BN-315661, BN-195402, BN-302868, BN-193625, Z-20303279, Z-20301172, BN-194158, BN-195269, VRAI-CLASSIC-4P, Z-19957449, Z-20046552
