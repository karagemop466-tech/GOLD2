F-001 · 16 of 19 listings never use the words "solid gold" high checklist gap

UPDATED 2026-10-22 (session S-75094819). The buying checklist requires the listing itself to say 'solid gold', not plated / vermeil / gold-filled / overlay / gold-tone. 16 of the 19 captured listings state a karat (10K/14K/18K) but never use the phrase 'solid gold'. The 3 exceptions are the Stone and Strand rings added in this pass (SS-CLARA, SS-RITA, SS-MIA): each product page carries a 'Solid Gold' badge and the sentence 'set in 14K solid gold or platinum'. Absence was checked in the retrieved page text for each item. Absence of the phrase is a documentation gap, not proof that a ring is plated — and presence of the phrase is not proof of purity either: all three Stone and Strand rings are final sale (F-016), so they cannot be returned after an XRF test.

Recommended action: Ask each seller in writing to confirm 'solid gold, not plated or filled' and keep the reply with the order record.

F-002 · No listing states a hallmark or maker's mark high checklist gap

The checklist asks for a 10K/14K/18K stamp PLUS a maker's/manufacturer's mark. None of the 19 product pages states what is stamped inside the band, and none names a manufacturer's mark. Blue Nile and VRAI do publish stock/style numbers, which is not the same thing as a physical hallmark. The 3 Stone and Strand pages added 2026-10-22 are no different: no stamp, no maker's mark, and not even a SKU in the retrieved text.

Recommended action: On arrival, photograph the inside of the band and have an independent jeweller read the stamp and maker's mark under magnification.

F-015 · Setting-only prices and complete-ring prices sit in the same tables high comparability

8 Blue Nile rows, the Brilliant Earth row and the VRAI row are 'Setting Price' / 'Setting Only' - the center diamond is extra. The 4 Zales rows and the 3 Stone and Strand rows are complete rings including diamonds. A $1,000 setting and a $900 complete ring are not comparable, and price-per-gram cannot be computed where the diamond dominates. Every row carries a price_basis label so the two groups can be separated.

Recommended action: Compare setting prices with setting prices only; add a centre-stone quote before comparing with a complete ring.

F-016 · All 3 Stone and Strand rings are final sale / non-returnable — they cannot be returned after independent testing high policy

Each of the three Stone and Strand product pages retrieved on 2026-10-22 shows 'Final Sale — This product is non-returnable' (for Rita and Mia it is also in the Product Description bullets verbatim), on the same page that advertises '100 Day Returns'. The retailer's returns policy confirms the exclusion: 'Any piece marked as final sale on the product page or purchased at a discount of 50% or greater' cannot be returned or exchanged. Rita (60% off) and Mia (50% off) are non-returnable on the discount rule alone. Consequence for this project's method: the receive → independent XRF test → return if it fails sequence cannot be followed for these three rings at these prices. They are published here because they are the first captured listings to say 'solid gold' — not as recommendations.

Recommended action: Do not buy these three rings as listed if post-delivery verification matters — there is no return path. If buying from Stone and Strand, choose an item that is not marked final sale and is discounted less than 50%, and get written confirmation before paying that it can be returned after independent testing.

Affected rows: SS-CLARA, SS-RITA, SS-MIA
