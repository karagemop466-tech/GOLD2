How to use this honestly

  • Do not use gross ring weight if it includes diamonds, gemstones, platinum prongs, or other non-gold components.
  • Keep setting/configurator and complete-ring prices separate. A price-to-melt ratio does not appraise craftsmanship, stones, brand, duties, fees, or resale spread.
  • A result below or above the asking price is only an arithmetic observation for review, not proof of fraud or value.
  • Verify weight and purity with an independent jeweller under the seller’s written return terms before wearing or resizing.
