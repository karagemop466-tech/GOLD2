How to use this honestly

  • A setting-only price can never be compared to melt — the diamond is not in either number, but the making charge is.
  • The ratio is only meaningful when the weight is real. Ask the seller for the gram weight and verify it on a jeweller's scale.
  • Melt is a floor, not a fair price. A ring at 2× melt is normal retail; a ring at 1.05× melt with a “14K” stamp is a warning sign.
  • Never use melt value as a resale expectation — scrap buyers pay below spot.
