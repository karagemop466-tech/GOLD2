## 2. Open findings

### R6-01 — HIGH — Four Zales rows publish unsupported yellow-gold fields

**Rows:** Z-19957449, Z-20046552, Z-20303279, Z-20301172.

Each independently retrieved official title says **“10K Gold”**, not “10K Yellow Gold.” The item evidence sections likewise support 10K but not Yellow. Nevertheless, `metal` is populated as `10K Yellow Gold` and `color` as `Yellow`. The shared-file QA does not detect this.

**Required disposition:** leave `color` blank and use only the supported metal wording unless an official product specification explicitly stating yellow is retrieved and quoted. Do not infer color from appearance, a URL, a category, or another product. Keep the numeric prices and 10K observations. Add a negative regression test that rejects an unsupported color.

### R6-02 — MEDIUM — Some identifiers and product notes are URL-derived or unquoted

- VRAI's display SKU is `classic-4-prong-dome/round-brilliant-yellow-gold`, but no official stock/style identifier was retrieved; this is URL-slug text.
- Four Zales SKU values occur in product URLs but not in the recorded product-text quotes. A URL identifier can be retained as a URL identifier, but should not be presented as a quoted manufacturer SKU without evidence.
- Z-20046552's note says the cluster is “a composite of round diamonds.” The retrieved text supports “composite of diamonds” and a cushion shape for the composite outline, not round constituent stones.

**Required disposition:** label URL identifiers explicitly or blank the SKU field; remove or officially support the round-stone note. Add SKU/notes to field-level QA instead of checking only price/weight/width/karat.

### R6-03 — HIGH — Solid-gold and verification language exceeds the evidence

- BN-194548's note says rhodium-finished white gold “is still a solid 14K gold alloy underneath.” The official page supports 14K white gold, rhodium finish, and platinum prongs; it does not establish the asserted solid-alloy conclusion. This conflicts with the same record's `solid_gold_explicit: false` and F-001.
- Every generated page's metadata says **“Verified solid-gold engagement ring research”** even though the dashboard says 16/16 listings lack explicit solid-gold wording and all physical verification remains outstanding.
- README language that three published details “decide whether a ring is really solid gold” is too strong. Listing wording, marks, and weight are documentation gates; they do not prove purity. Independent professional testing is still required.

**Required disposition:** remove the BN-194548 inference; change metadata to “solid-gold research with unresolved verification” or equivalent; describe the three bullets as evidence to request, not authenticity proof. Keep the independent XRF/professional-test warning prominent.

### R6-04 — MEDIUM — The blanket card-acceptance statement is contradicted by official pages

F-012 and the landing checklist say card acceptance is not stated on any captured page. Independently retrieved Blue Nile product pages explicitly display offers **“with the Blue Nile Credit Card.”** This does not establish every card network or protection outcome, but it does contradict the blanket absence claim.

**Required disposition:** record the exact Blue Nile card offer with its product/policy source and scope. Keep other retailers and general card-network acceptance blank unless verified. Do not equate a financing offer with a particular chargeback outcome.

### R6-05 — MEDIUM — Policy/contact evidence is incomplete or mismatched

- [VRAI returns](https://www.vrai.com/returns-exchanges) states a 30-day eligible-item window, a recycling-fee deduction, a **$300 engagement-ring fee**, store credit for items above $15,000, noneligibility for modified/resized and custom-designed items, and free return shipping for the US/Canada/EU/UK. The dataset honestly leaves several fields blank, but the landing claim that resized/custom exclusions are recorded per retailer is not true.
- [Brilliant Earth's care hub](https://www.brilliantearth.com/weve-got-you-covered/) states free 30-day returns/exchanges, free shipping both ways, and free one-year resizing, while the record's return window is blank.
- Brilliant Earth's record cites `/contact/` for phone `1.415.426.2781`; the independently retrieved [contact page](https://www.brilliantearth.com/contact/) prominently publishes `800.691.0952`. The recorded number did appear in the localized product-page header, so the problem is source/region citation, not a fabricated digit string.

**Required disposition:** retrieve and quote the relevant official policy text, populate only supported fields with region/scope, preserve exclusions and fees, and make each contact citation support its value. Until then, change the landing summary to say exclusions are incomplete and flagged.

### R6-06 — MEDIUM — VRAI's size-specific weight loses its context in category tables

The source and `weight_basis` correctly say **2.86 g at ring size 6**. `tools/build_site.py` never renders `weight_basis`, so category pages show simply “2.86 g.” The price page does retain the size context, proving this is a rendering omission rather than a source error.

**Required disposition:** display “2.86 g (size 6)” wherever that weight appears and carry variant/size context into any item-specific calculation. Never generalize it to all sizes.

### R6-07 — HIGH — Calculator accepts a risky weight basis, ambiguous currency, and nonfinite input

- “Ring weight (g)” is multiplied by karat/24 without saying to exclude gemstones and non-gold parts. For a complete ring, gross weight can overstate nominal fine gold.
- “Asking price (optional)” has no currency even though the catalog contains EUR and the quote is USD. A EUR ask can therefore be compared numerically with a USD theoretical value.
- `parseFloat(...) > 0` does not reject `Infinity`. Executing the actual built script with `w=Infinity`, `k=14`, and the published quote produced **`Infinity g` and `$∞`**.

**Required disposition:** require total **metal-only** weight, explicitly label the ask currency, reject nonfinite/nonpositive inputs with a visible validation message and blank results, and add Infinity/NaN/cross-currency negative tests. Keep unknown inputs blank.

### R6-08 — MEDIUM — Retail/scrap rules and “melt floor” claims lack sources

The calculator publishes “2× melt is normal retail,” “1.05× melt … is a warning sign,” “1.0× … would mean sold at scrap,” and “Melt is a floor.” No authoritative dated source or methodology supports these market generalizations. A theoretical spot-ratio calculation cannot establish an actual refiner bid, retail norm, fraud threshold, or resale floor.

**Required disposition:** remove these rules or provide authoritative, scoped, dated evidence and uncertainty. Rename “melt value” to “theoretical fine-gold spot value” where appropriate and state that fees, stones, workmanship, taxes, and refiner terms are excluded.

### R6-09 — HIGH — The supplied QA PASS has material false-negative paths

`tools/qa.py` is useful but its report overstates coverage:

- “Every value traces to a verbatim quote” checks that each string in `evidence_quotes` occurs in a shared evidence file; it does not map every populated field to a quote.
- Numeric backing searches the entire shared category evidence file, so another row or rejected snippet can satisfy a substring check.
- Color, SKU, notes, retailer policy summaries, and several blank-field cases are not field-checked. R6-01/R6-02 are concrete false negatives.
- Five blank band widths have no width-specific flag despite README's blanket statement that missing values are flagged.
- Link checking validates local targets but does not prove that external official URLs remain live or that `href="#fragment"` targets exist.

**Required disposition:** use item-scoped structured evidence keyed by field, reject matches in rejected-value sections, add mutation/negative fixtures, validate anchors, and label the result “automated consistency checks passed” rather than proof that every value is true. Keep official-page retrieval and independent review mandatory after a PASS.

Brand recognition and a seller's own “secure shopping” text do not establish reputation. The landing “Records” item covers research URLs/dates/quotes, not the buyer's invoice, seller replies, hallmark/maker's-mark photographs, appraisal/certificate, and test report.

**Required disposition:** either add a scoped seller-reputation assessment using appropriate independent sources or label reputation “not independently assessed.” Add a purchase-documentation checklist and keep those records distinct from product-truth evidence.
