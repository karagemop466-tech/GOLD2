12-Point Solid Gold Online Buying Checklist

Checklist CriterionVerification StandardOfficial Authority
01. Gold purity (karat)Confirm exact karat — `10K` (41.67%), `14K` (58.33%), `18K` (75.00%), `22K` (91.67%), or `24K` (99.99–100%). Higher karat means higher pure gold content and melt floor.Official reference ↗
02. Explicit 'solid gold' wordingRequire explicit words like `14K Solid Gold` or `Solid 18k Gold` on the official product page — reject `gold-plated`, `vermeil`, `gold-filled`, `gold overlay`, `hollow`, or `gold-tone`.Official reference ↗
03. Hallmark + maker's markUnder the National Stamping Act (`15 U.S.C. § 297`), any quality mark (`14K`, `750`, etc.) in interstate commerce must be accompanied by the registered manufacturer's trademark.Official reference ↗
04. Ring weight in grams (metal-only)Check metal mass in metric grams (`g`), separated from gemstone carats (`1 ct = 0.200 g`). Ultra-light bands (<1.8 g metal) bend or snap easily even when solid 14K.Official reference ↗
05. Seller reputation & verified contactCross-check independent BBB accreditation, customer reviews, 3-year complaint narratives, street address, phone, and email before paying.Official reference ↗
06. Price vs raw gold melt valueCompare retail price against `metal_g × (karat/24) × ($3,995.47 / 31.1034768)`. Any 'solid gold' ring priced near or below raw melt value is an immediate counterfeit/plating red flag.Official reference ↗
07. Return policy & resizing trapsConfirm a clear 14–100 day return window for full refund and check exclusions on resized, custom, made-to-order, engraved, or >=50%-off Final Sale items.Official reference ↗
08. Credit card purchase protectionAlways pay by major credit card (never bank wire, crypto, Zelle, or cash transfer) to preserve statutory billing-error and chargeback rights.Official reference ↗
09. Save written pre-purchase documentationScreenshot the full dated listing URL, karat, gram weight, hallmark statement, invoice specs, and support chat confirmation before ordering.Official reference ↗
10. Post-arrival independent jeweler inspectionImmediately upon delivery — before wearing, resizing, or removing tags — have an independent local jeweler inspect stone seating, stamps, and exact gram weight.Official reference ↗
11. Non-destructive XRF purity assayAsk an independent jeweler, coin dealer, or assay lab to test alloy composition via non-destructive X-ray fluorescence (`XRF`) rather than acid filing.Official reference ↗
12. Walk-away ruleNever buy from any seller that refuses written gram specs, forbids independent post-delivery XRF/scale inspection, or sells uninspected pieces as non-returnable Final Sale.Official reference ↗
