12-Point Solid Gold Online Buying Checklist

Checklist CriterionVerification StandardOfficial Authority
01. Gold purity (karat)Confirm the exact stated karat (10K, 14K, 18K, 22K, or 24K). Karat/24 is a nominal calculation ratio, not an assay; seller-declared decimal fineness is a separate input.Official reference ↗
02. Explicit 'solid gold' wordingRequire explicit solid-gold wording on the exact product page. Do not substitute plating, vermeil, gold-filled, overlay, or gold-tone wording, and do not infer solid gold from karat alone.Official reference ↗
03. Hallmark + maker's markLook for a karat quality mark and the accompanying manufacturer responsibility/trademark evidence required by the cited marking rules. A stamp alone does not prove actual purity.Official reference ↗
04. Ring weight in grams (metal-only)Request the selected-size metal weight in grams, excluding stones and non-gold components. Never infer grams from bandwidth or substitute gemstone carats; 1 ct = 0.200 g is a mass conversion, not a purity test.Official reference ↗
05. Seller reputation & contactResearch independent reviews and complaints and verify real contact information. Review scores and allegations are not authenticity findings; no call or email is treated as completed unless documented.Official reference ↗
06. Price vs raw gold melt valueFor a documented metal-only weight, compare the exact configuration's native-currency price with conditional `metal_g × nominal_fineness × dated_spot / 31.1034768` arithmetic. An unusually cheap price is a review flag, not proof of plating or a guaranteed resale floor.Official reference ↗
07. Return policy & resizing trapsConfirm the exact order's return deadline, refund method, fees, and exclusions for resizing, customization, engraving, alteration, and final sale. Do not substitute a generic banner for order-specific eligibility.Official reference ↗
08. Credit card purchase protectionPrefer a credit card for applicable purchase protection and avoid irreversible payment methods. Card-network acceptance and dispute outcomes are not guaranteed.Official reference ↗
09. Save written pre-purchase documentationPreserve the dated listing, URL, selected configuration, seller claims, receipt, and correspondence. Screenshots support later comparison but do not authenticate the metal.Official reference ↗
10. Post-arrival independent jeweler inspectionBefore wearing, resizing, engraving, or removing tags, ask an independent jeweler to inspect the piece and verify its weight and markings. Do not perform a test that voids return eligibility.Official reference ↗
11. Professional purity testingAsk for XRF or another professional gold-purity assessment. Surface XRF can be misleading for gold-over-gold construction, so a professional must decide whether additional testing is needed.Official reference ↗
12. Walk-away ruleDo not buy if the seller will not permit independent verification and a qualifying return. Missing evidence remains a reason to ask questions, not permission to invent an answer.Official reference ↗
