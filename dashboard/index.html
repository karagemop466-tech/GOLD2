⛔ NO HALLUCINATIONS — read this first

Every price, spec, and policy claim on this site must trace to an official source linked on the same row. If a value could not be confirmed on an official page, the cell is left blank and flagged unverified. We never infer, estimate, or fill in values. Blank does not mean zero — it means “not confirmed.”
