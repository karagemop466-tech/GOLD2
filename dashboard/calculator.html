This calculator stores no prices. You enter a current USD-per-troy-ounce quote and its date/time; the math is shown step by step so you can check it. Get the benchmark method at the LBMA Precious Metal Prices page.

Gold weight / melt-value calculator

Melt (raw-gold) value tells you what the gold content alone is worth — one input for checklist item 6 (price vs. gold value). It is not a retail valuation: craftsmanship, stones, brand, and margin are extra, and this spot-price arithmetic is theoretical — actual buy/sell spreads differ. Per the project checklist, an unusually cheap asking price is a red flag; finished jewelry normally prices far above melt.

Exclude stones and non-gold components — total ring weight overstates gold content. Use the seller's stated metal weight or a jeweler's measurement.

Currency: USD only. Convert other currencies first (not done here). Only finite positive numbers are accepted — anything else blanks the outputs.

Formulas (check our math)

pure-gold grams   = metal weight (g) × karat ÷ 24
price per gram    = spot (USD/troy oz) ÷ 31.1034768
melt value (USD)  = pure-gold grams × price per gram

Constants: 1 troy ounce = 31.1034768 g exact — LBMA, The Troy Ounce · NIST precious-metals conversions. Benchmark method (no snapshot stored here): LBMA Precious Metal Prices.

Worked example (HYPOTHETICAL — not a real ring): 4.50 g of metal at 14K with a $4,000/troy-oz quote → pure gold = 4.50 × 14 ÷ 24 = 2.625 g; per-gram = 4000 ÷ 31.1034768 ≈ $128.60; melt ≈ 2.625 × 128.60 ≈ $337.58. Labeled hypothetical so it can never be mistaken for a finding.
