DF-002 · Factory Direct Jewelry `FDJ-GR0001`: Ultra-Light `1.25 g` Displayed Weight vs `1.37 ctw` Diamonds & `1.8/5` BBB Score

Although FDJ-GR0001 explicitly states 'Made with solid gold. Never plated.' and 'Karat stamped inside the band.', its default displayed weight is 'Weight:1.25 Grams' alongside 1.37 carats of lab diamonds (0.274 g gem mass). Recommended Buyer Action: Treat verbal 'solid gold + stamped' claims as insufficient whenever gram mass is ultra-low or BBB complaints report snapped shanks.

MEDIUM SEVERITY

DF-004 · Sarraf Jewelry Inc. (`SAR-*`): Internal Policy Contradictions & Size-10 Weight Benchmark Variance

Sarraf publishes explicit '14K Solid Gold', 'US$' prices, and separate 'Approximate Metal Weight' in grams, and holds an A+ Accredited BBB profile since 1998. However, live audit caught: (1) product tab allows engraved ring returns less $25 fee while /about-sarraf/ says 'non-engraved items'; (2) warranty deductible reads '$100 or 10%' on diamond ring pages vs '$50 or 5%' on 10K bands; (3) SAR-144773's 13.40 g weight ($1,011.39 melt = 74.3% of US$1,362) is benchmarked at ring size 10, weighing less in smaller sizes (10.1–10.2 g reported in buyer reviews; BBB customer reviews 2.33/5).

Recommended Buyer Action: Confirm exact gram weight for your ring size in writing before ordering, avoid engraving before XRF/scale inspection, and pay by credit card.

MEDIUM SEVERITY

DF-005 · Gross Item Weight vs Stone-Free Metal Weight (`AG-SAPPHIRE-RING`, `AV-BRIDGE`, `AV-CLEAN`)

Automic Gold stone-free bands (`AG-COFFIN-RING` 6.1g, `AG-24K` 11.0g) give pure metal weight, whereas gemstone rings (`AG-SAPPHIRE-RING` 1.2g) include gemstone mass, and Auvere 22K rings (`AV-BRIDGE` 5–6.1g, `AV-CLEAN` 2.8–3.7g) publish size ranges rather than a single size weight.

Recommended Buyer Action: Use the gemstone carat deduction input (`1 ct = 0.200 g`) in the calculator when weighing set rings; never plug gross stone+metal mass directly into pure alloy melt.

Official Sources: https://www.automicgold.com/products/sapphire-ring ↗ · https://auvere.com/product/diamond-bridge-ring-tall/ ↗
