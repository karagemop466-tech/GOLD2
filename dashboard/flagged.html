F-SOLID-WORDING — 'Solid gold' wording not observed on listing pages

Applies to: BN-001 BN-002 BN-003 BN-004 CS-001

Issue: Listings state the metal (e.g., '14K Yellow Gold') but the fetched page text does not include the words 'solid gold'. The project checklist requires an explicit 'solid gold' statement (not plated/vermeil/filled/overlay/tone).

Evidence: Fetched page text for all five products (2026-10-22). FTC 16 CFR 23.3 permits karat+'Gold' marking and separately bars calling hollow-center items 'solid' — see reference page.

How to resolve: Ask the seller in writing whether the ring is solid gold throughout (and solid vs. hollow construction), and keep the reply with the receipt. Do not treat karat metal text as proof of solid construction.

F-SPOT-PRICE — No gold spot-price snapshot stored (by design)

Applies to: calculator all-rings

Issue: Gold prices move constantly; any stored snapshot would go stale and risk becoming a hallucinated 'current price'. This site stores no spot price.

Evidence: LBMA publishes the benchmark twice daily on business days and requires a licence for data redistribution — see reference page.

How to resolve: Enter a current USD-per-troy-ounce figure yourself in the calculator from the LBMA page or your broker, and note its date/time. Compare the setting price against the computed melt value as one input — not as a valuation.
