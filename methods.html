Method & QA

This page is designed to make the dataset challengeable. It distinguishes retrieved facts, rejected values, calculated values, independent profile data, and physical verification that has not occurred.

1. The no-hallucination rule

Publish only what a retrieved source supports. Each row stores an official URL, UTC retrieval date, per-item evidence file, and field-specific quote lists. A missing or ambiguous value is null and flagged. Null never means zero, false, or a negative physical finding. No price is inferred from position, discount arithmetic, currency conversion, or a search snippet.

2. Source hierarchy and scope

  • Product facts and seller policies: the retailer’s own product, policy, payment, and contact pages.
  • Independent reputation context: BBB profile/review/complaint pages with dated counts, identity scope, and BBB disclaimers. Allegations are not treated as proven; combined-parent and candidate profiles are not silently relabeled.
  • Gold reference: World Gold Council spot page with quote date and ICE Data Services attribution.
  • Calculation constants/context: NIST troy-ounce conversion chart and eCFR gold-labeling guidance.
  • Rejected as product evidence: search snippets, aggregators, blogs, coupon pages, unrelated page text, and values from other items in the same category.

3. Field-scoped evidence

The current audit resolves 277 field-specific quotes across 16 item files, four retailer files, and the gold/calculation evidence. A price, width, or karat in one item’s file cannot support another item. Shared category files are retained only as inherited audit history and are not authoritative inputs.

8. What to do with a flagged row

  1. Open the official source and item evidence; re-read the exact field and price basis.
  2. Ask the seller in writing about solid-gold construction, metal-only gram weight, physical hallmark/maker’s mark, and independent non-destructive testing permission.
  3. Keep the written response and the dated checkout record. Do not rely on a cached snippet or an unlabeled amount.
  4. Before wearing or resizing, use an independent jeweller for XRF, markings, and weight, subject to the seller’s return terms.
  5. If the seller refuses independent verification or a return, do not buy.
