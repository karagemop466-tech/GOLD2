F-021

Policy details that change the return decision

Blue Nile

Return exclusions: Personalized/custom/engraved items, items resized after initial purchase, Diamond Upgrade Program purchases, clearance and Final Sale items are excluded.

Resize terms: Eligible rings: one free resize, up or down two sizes, within 6 months of purchase if the design permits.

Return shipping: Up to two complimentary returns per customer/year; alternate carriers or returns outside that service can incur fees

Payment scope: US policy: major credit cards; Visa/Mastercard debit only; PayPal below $50,000; wire transfer; Affirm and Splitit financing.

Read field-scoped policy evidence

Zales

Return exclusions: Custom designs and jewelry engraved or altered/modified beyond sizing or stone setting are excluded. Separate Zales PDF: rings resized at a Zales store, another retailer, or independent jeweler are not accepted for online refund/exchange; rings purchased and sized from the online store are accepted.

Resize terms: Online-purchased and online-sized rings may be returned/exchanged; store, other-retailer, and independent-jeweler resizing is not accepted online. Sized-to-order ring sizing fee is refunded on return.

Return shipping: Free pre-printed label for online returns by mail

Payment scope: Official payment-page image alt text lists card and digital-wallet methods; Zales card, Progressive Leasing, and Affirm are separate financing options.

Read field-scoped policy evidence

VRAI

Return exclusions: Modified/resized rings, third-party alterations, custom designs, and other listed items are non-eligible. Below $15,000: store credit or refund less recycling fee; above $15,000: store credit only. Engagement-ring recycling fee is $300, waived for store credit.

Resize terms: One complimentary resize up to one full US size, by quarter/half/full size; other restrictions apply.

Return shipping: Free in the US; other destination rules are stated separately

Payment scope: PayPal and credit cards accepted; some countries offer Klarna invoice, direct debit, or bank transfer.

Read field-scoped policy evidence

Brilliant Earth

Return exclusions: Resized/modified and custom-designed items are excluded. Engraved rings may be returned with a $40 engraving-removal charge; other exclusions apply.

Resize terms: One complimentary resizing within 60 days regardless of sizing range, or up to 1 year within sizing range; restrictions and fees apply.

Return shipping: Shipping covered both ways, subject to policy instructions

Payment scope: Major cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, and bank wire; Affirm and Wells Fargo are financing options with eligibility/terms.

Read field-scoped policy evidence

Automic Gold

Return exclusions: Engraved items, custom items, and modified items are final sale.

Resize terms: Paid resizing starting at $50–$60; modified items become final sale.

Return shipping: Free US return shipping; mail within 14 days to avoid a recycling fee

Payment scope: Official FAQ lists all major credit & debit cards, PayPal, Shop Pay, Affirm, AfterPay, Google Pay, and Apple Pay.

Read field-scoped policy evidence

Mejuri

Return exclusions: Engraved and monogrammed products, piercing stud earrings, gift cards, and final sale items are excluded; post-purchase resize/custom exclusions are unstated.

Resize terms: None

Return shipping: Free 30-day returns in the US, Canada, UK, Australia, and Germany

Payment scope: Official FAQ lists credit cards accepted: Visa, Mastercard, and American Express.

Read field-scoped policy evidence

Sarraf Jewelry Inc.

Return exclusions: Items returned must not have been worn, altered, or damaged; custom or special order items excluded; internal policy conflict on engraved items ($25 removal fee vs non-engraved rule, F-036).

Resize terms: Ring sizing may require additional processing days prior to shipping.

Return shipping: All shipping and handling costs are the sole responsibility of the consumer.

Payment scope: Major credit cards accepted (refunds credited within 7 business days of receipt); always use credit card rather than check/money order.

Read field-scoped policy evidence

Factory Direct Jewelry

Return exclusions: Engraved, custom-made, or personalized items are non-returnable; altered items carry a 20% restocking fee; third-party jeweler work automatically voids warranty.

Resize terms: Any modification, repair, sizing, stone setting, or work performed by another jeweler or third party automatically voids this warranty.

Return shipping: customer is responsible for return shipping costs

Payment scope: Major credit cards accepted; BBB review history includes disputed post-chargeback billing — monitor credit card statements closely (F-035).

Read field-scoped policy evidence

Stone and Strand

Return exclusions: All items marked at 50% off or more are considered Final Sale and are non-returnable; Made-to-order, custom, and engraved pieces are final sale.

Resize terms: Stone and Strand does not offer complimentary resizing; altered pieces are excluded from return.

Return shipping: $12.50 restocking fee

Payment scope: Major credit cards accepted, but >=50%-off Final Sale items cannot be returned after an independent XRF check (F-034).

Read field-scoped policy evidence

Costco Wholesale

Return exclusions: Diamonds: 1.00ct or larger require original IGI/GIA certificate and gemological verification prior to refund; third-party resizing alters original condition.

Resize terms: Costco does not resize rings; independent resizing alters original condition.

Return shipping: None

Payment scope: Online accepts Visa and Mastercard; US warehouse registers accept Visa credit cards (F-038).

Read field-scoped policy evidence
Safe sequence: obtain written permission for independent non-destructive inspection; keep the item unworn and unaltered; inspect and weigh it; then follow the exact seller/channel policy. Do not resize until you have decided to keep the ring and understand the return/warranty consequences.
