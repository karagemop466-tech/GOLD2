Why the exclusions matter

A resize can void the return right. Blue Nile excludes items resized after initial purchase and custom orders from returns, and offers one free resize within six months. The safe order is therefore: receive → independent XRF test and weighing → only then resize. If the test is refused or the ring fails, return it untouched.

Marketplace note

James Allen product URLs now redirect to Blue Nile and at least one cached James Allen ring (item 41515) rendered as "This popular style is sold out". James Allen designs appear inside the Blue Nile catalogue, so the brand on the ring and the company you buy from are no longer the same. See flag F-009.
