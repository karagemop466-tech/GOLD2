Method, not an appraisal

Nominal fine-gold grams = verified gold-alloy-only grams × listed karat ÷ 24. Contained gold value = nominal fine-gold grams × verified USD per fine-gold gram. Stones, non-gold components and gross ring weights must not be counted as gold alloy. A retail setting price cannot substitute for metal weight.

Derived figures would be labeled calculations, not source facts. This does not estimate resale payout, workmanship, gemstones, fees or taxes. A listed karat is still a seller claim, not a laboratory result.
