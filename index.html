What this project checks

  • 01Purity. Karat is quoted only where the retailer states it. It is a seller claim, not an XRF result.
  • 02Solid gold. 16 of 16 item-specific captures do not state the required phrase; all remain flagged (F-001).
  • 03Hallmark. 16 of 16 rows lack documented physical karat/maker’s marks; stock and source IDs are not hallmarks (F-002).
  • 04Weight. Metal-only grams are published for 1 of 16 rows; every other weight is blank (F-003).
  • 05Seller. Official contact, payment, and return policies are separated from scoped independent BBB profile data. Neither is an endorsement.
  • 06Price. 13 rows have explicitly assigned prices; 3 are blank, including 2 unlabeled Zales displays. 3 cached-price discrepancies are logged as rejected evidence.
  • 07Returns. Resized/custom exclusions, VRAI fees, Brilliant Earth engraving terms, and Zales resizing-channel rules are shown per retailer.
  • 08Payment. Accepted methods are linked to official payment/terms pages. Acceptance does not prove chargeback or purchase-protection outcomes.
  • 09Records. Every row links its official product page and its own field-scoped evidence file with retrieval date.
  • 10On arrival. No item was physically tested. Obtain written testing permission, then use independent non-destructive XRF and weighing before wearing or resizing.
A karat description or stamp alone does not prove purity. Seller documentation, correct markings, and independent physical testing answer different questions; none is silently substituted for another.
