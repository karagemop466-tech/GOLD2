What this project checks

  • 01Purity. Karat is recorded only where the retailer states it on the page. 10K, 14K and 18K rows are kept separate.
  • 02Solid gold. The listing must say solid gold. 16 of 16 captured listings state a karat but never that phrase — see F-001.
  • 03Hallmark. Karat stamp plus maker's mark. 16 of 16 listings state neither — see F-002.
  • 04Weight. Grams. Published for 1 of 16 items; blank everywhere else — see F-003.
  • 05Seller. 4 retailers with official pages; contact details recorded only where published.
  • 06Price. Dated page displays observed on 2026-10-22; no live feed with its basis (setting-only vs complete ring). Cached snippet prices were rejected in 3 cases.
  • 07Returns. Policy coverage is incomplete. Confirm the exact order, fees, resized/custom exclusions and inspection permission — see F-010/F-011.
  • 08Payment. Blue Nile Credit Card offers are displayed; accepted networks and issuer protection terms remain unconfirmed — see F-012.
  • 09Records. Keep research URL/date/evidence plus your receipt, exact selected configuration, seller replies, certificates, paired-mark photos and test report.
  • 10On arrival. Independent professional weight/purity assessment before wear or resizing, within written return permission; surface XRF alone can mislead.
The key rule: a 14K or 18K stamp alone does not prove purity. No seller is certified trustworthy here. Require independent professional assessment and a qualifying return before purchase; see the expanded buying checklist and GIA testing caveat.
