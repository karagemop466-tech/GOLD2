What this project checks

  • 01Purity. Karat is recorded only where the retailer states it on the page. 10K, 14K and 18K rows are kept separate.
  • 02Solid gold. The listing must say solid gold. 16 of 16 captured listings state a karat but never that phrase — see F-001.
  • 03Hallmark. Karat stamp plus maker's mark. 16 of 16 listings state neither — see F-002.
  • 04Weight. Grams. Published for 1 of 16 items; blank everywhere else — see F-003.
  • 05Seller. 4 retailers with official pages; contact details recorded only where published.
  • 06Price. Live price captured on 2026-10-22 with its basis (setting-only vs complete ring). Cached snippet prices were rejected in 3 cases.
  • 07Returns. Window and the resized/custom exclusions recorded per retailer — see F-010.
  • 08Payment. Card acceptance is not stated on any captured page; left blank — see F-012.
  • 09Records. Listing URL, retrieval date and verbatim evidence kept in evidence/ for every row.
  • 10On arrival. Independent jeweller check before wearing or resizing; ask for XRF.
The key rule: a 14K or 18K stamp alone does not prove purity. Reputable seller + correct markings + sensible price + independent XRF test after delivery is the only sequence that holds up.
